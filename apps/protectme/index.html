Android App

Built to hold the line on your phone

Choose which apps to block completely, cut specific apps off from the internet using a local on-device VPN, and optionally block Google Search. While protection is active, ProtectMe guards against being switched off or uninstalled in a weak moment.

Windows Companion · Coming Soon

Hold to protect — instant lock on Windows

The same clean, distraction-free protection on Windows 11. Hold to activate protection and switch from Unprotected to Protected with a single deliberate action — no clutter, no endless menus.

Unlock Keys & Anti-Tamper

Lock it behind a key you choose

Set a password — or pair a physical QR code, barcode, or NFC tag on Android — so turning protection off takes real intent. Let a trusted friend or family member hold the key when you need extra accountability.

Rules Hub

Blocked apps, offline mode & schedules

Manage everything in one place: pick apps to block outright, choose apps to take offline without cutting off the rest of your device, and set recurring schedules so protection turns on automatically when you need it most.

Website Protection

Safe Browsing, porn blocking & custom rules

Enable Safe Browsing, block adult websites, block images and video where needed, and manage your own website and webpage rules so your browser stays aligned with your goals.

100% On-Device Privacy

Your device. Your data. Nobody else's.

Everything ProtectMe does happens locally on your own device. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no servers collecting your browsing or app activity.
