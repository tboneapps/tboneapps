No Algorithm

A clear sky, not a swirl of suggestions

No recommendation engine, no autoplay, no "up next." When the algorithm's noise fades, what's left is just the channels you actually chose — clear, in order, undisturbed.

Open RSS, No Account

Follow any channel, quietly

Built on open RSS feeds rather than a locked-down API. No sign-in, no tracking, no ads deciding what surfaces. You pick the channel — Focus Feed just shows you what they've posted, nothing more.

Bedtime Mode

Watch & Sleep, or Listen & Sleep

Set a cut-off and the app locks itself down for the night — video keeps playing quietly in the background if you choose Listen & Sleep, or stops cleanly at your chosen time. No scrolling into 2am by accident.

Picture-in-Picture

Keep watching, keep moving

Shrink the video into a floating window and carry on with your day — reply to a message, check your list, switch apps. The channel you're watching stays with you, quietly, in the corner.

Keyword Filtering

Let some things through, not others

Filter out topics you don't want cluttering your feed by keyword or phrase. What matters to you stays lit up. What doesn't, quietly fades before it ever reaches you.
