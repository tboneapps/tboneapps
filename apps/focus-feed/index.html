Watch what you chose to watch.Nothing else.

Focus Feed follows your favourite YouTube channels through their own open RSS feeds — no algorithm deciding what's next, no autoplay dragging you sideways, no recommended rabbit holes. Just the channels you follow, in the order they posted.

Videos & Shorts, Kept Separate

Switch cleanly with one tap

Inside any channel, long-form Videos and Shorts live in their own separate tabs — a clean chronological grid with no autoplay pulling you from one clip to the next.

Playlists & Collections

Build your own video collection

Organize videos and channels into custom playlist folders — Movies, Relax/Sleep, TV Series, or anything you like. Add a full YouTube playlist at once and keep everything neatly sorted.

Keyword Filtering

Only the titles you want

Filter any channel by keywords — or hide Videos or Shorts completely. Only new uploads matching what you care about make it into your feed; everything else stays out of sight.

Track & Watch Your Way

Start from the first video. Hide what you've watched.

Sort any channel Recently Added, Oldest Added, or A–Z, tick off videos as you watch them, and hide watched episodes so you can enjoy a channel start to finish without losing your place.

Smart Bedtime Mode Android only

Your screen turns off automatically

Set a timer (15m, 30m, 45m, 60m, custom) or choose Video End — Focus Feed winds down automatically so you never accidentally scroll into 2am.

Parent Controls & Self-Commitment

Protect yourself & loved ones

Lock channel additions behind a Parent PIN, use self-commitment locks, and set Daily Watch Limits — once time is up, watching pauses until more time is granted.

No Account Needed. Made for You

Settings that respect your time

Force subtitles off, customize upload alerts and check frequency, and keep your entire library safe with Google Drive cloud backup and restore — all with zero sign-in required.
