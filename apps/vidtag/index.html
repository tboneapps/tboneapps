VidTag runs entirely on your device. There's no upload step, no cloud processing, and no connection required to embed metadata into a video — it works just as well on a plane as it does at home.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add cover art to a video on Android?

Open the video in VidTag, tap Add Cover Art, choose a photo from your gallery, and save. VidTag embeds the artwork directly into the video file's metadata in seconds.

Can I edit video metadata without a computer?

Yes. VidTag is a phone-only Android app — there's no desktop software or cable required. Everything happens on-device in a couple of taps.

Does VidTag add a watermark?

No. VidTag never stamps a watermark, logo, or overlay onto your videos or their artwork — the file you export is clean.

Does VidTag need an internet connection?

No. VidTag reads and writes video metadata entirely offline, so it works on a plane, on airplane mode, or anywhere without signal.
