Questions

Does it work for both UK and US drivers?

Yes. Choose United Kingdom (HMRC rates) or United States (IRS rates) in settings. UK users choose car, van, motorcycle or bicycle; US users choose business, medical or moving, or charitable mileage.

How is the 10,000-mile band handled for UK cars and vans?

Miles are counted per tax year, in date order. The first 10,000 miles earn the higher rate and anything after earns 25p. The live allowance on the map screen uses the same rule, so it matches your report.

Can I use it without a signal?

Yes. Download the area you drive in, then switch on “Use offline maps”. GPS tracking itself does not need data.

What is in the PDF report?

A summary page with totals and total claimable, your entries, optional trip maps (four per page), and expenses with receipts. Choose a full tax year, one month or a custom date range.

Is it free?

You can try Driver Notebook free. When you want unlimited tracking, unlock it for £10 a year through Google Play. You can cancel any time in Google Play.
