Driver Notebook Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Summary

Driver Notebook keeps your trips, expenses and receipts on your own phone. We do not run a server that collects your data, we have no accounts, and we do not show ads, sell your data or track you across other apps or websites.

Who we are

Driver Notebook is published by Tbone Apps. Contact: tboneapps@outlook.com

Information the app uses

  • Location. When you start a trip, the app records your GPS position to measure miles and draw the route. Android and iOS may ask for background location so a trip keeps recording while the screen is off. The app only records while a trip you started is running. Location is saved on your device and is not sent to us.
  • Trips and entries. Dates, distances, routes, start and end times, notes and vehicle details that you enter or record.
  • Expenses and receipts. Amounts, notes and any receipt photos you attach.
  • Photos and camera. Used only when you choose to take or pick a picture of a receipt or an odometer reading. Text reading from images happens on your device.
  • Settings. Your tax region (UK or US), currency, vehicle choice and similar preferences.

All of this is stored in the app's private storage on your device. We cannot see it.

Backups you choose to make

  • Android: Google Drive. If you choose Back up to Google Drive, the app saves one backup file in a private app-only folder in your own Google Drive. Only this app can see that folder. The app asks for no other access to your Drive.
  • iPhone: iCloud. If you choose iCloud backup, the backup file is saved in the app's private iCloud container in your own iCloud account.
  • Files you export. PDF reports and backup files you save or share go where you send them.

Backups are held by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies. We do not receive them. You can delete a backup from your Google Drive or iCloud storage settings at any time.

Maps

To show maps, the app downloads map tiles from OpenFreeMap, which uses data from OpenStreetMap. Like any web request, this tells the map server your device's IP address and which map area you are viewing. If you download offline maps, they are stored on your device and used without a connection. Your trip routes are not sent to the map server.

Subscriptions and payments

Driver Notebook is free to use up to a set number of entries, and a yearly subscription removes that limit. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your card or payment details. On Android and iPhone we use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active, which involves an anonymous app user identifier and purchase information. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, email address or phone number inside the app.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics tools in the app.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information.
  • We do not use your information to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • You can delete any trip, expense or receipt inside the app.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your device.
  • You can switch off location, camera or photo access at any time in your phone's settings. Trip recording needs location to work.
  • Delete any Google Drive or iCloud backup from your account's storage settings.

Because your data stays on your device and in your own cloud account, you control it directly. If you have a question about your data, email us.

Children

Driver Notebook is a tax and mileage tool for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: tboneapps@outlook.com or the contact page.
