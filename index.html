About Tbone Apps

Software for intentional technology use.

Tbone Apps develops software designed to help people use technology more intentionally. Our apps focus on digital wellbeing, reducing distractions, online safety, parental control, and helping families create healthier technology habits.

The goal is simple: make technology easier to manage, safer to use, and less likely to pull people into endless distraction.

Create playlist folders
