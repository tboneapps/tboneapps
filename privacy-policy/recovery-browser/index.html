Recovery Browser Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Recovery Browser: Porn Blocker ("Recovery Browser", "the app") collects, uses, and protects information. Recovery Browser is developed by Tbone Apps.

Recovery Browser

Developer: Tbone Apps

Contact: tboneapps@outlook.com

Effective date: 12 August 2026

What Recovery Browser Does

Recovery Browser is a whitelist-based web browser built to help people avoid pornography and stay accountable to their own recovery. It blocks non-whitelisted websites, blocks adult content, and includes two streak trackers ("Porn-Free" and "No Masturbation") so you can see how long it's been since you last gave in, along with a history of past streaks.

Information We Collect

In short: none of the data below ever leaves your device. We do not collect, transmit, or upload any of it to Tbone Apps or anyone else.

Streak history: Your Porn-Free and No Masturbation start times, "Gave In" resets, and past-streak history (start date, end date, duration) are stored locally on your device only.

Whitelist entries: The list of websites and pages you whitelist is stored locally on your device only.

Settings: Your Commitment Timer, Trusted Person Lock, Daily Browser Timer, and other preferences are stored locally on your device only.

Backup files: If you use Backup & Restore, the app creates a backup file at a location you choose using Android's own file picker. If you choose a cloud-connected location (for example, a Google Drive folder you already have set up on your device), that file is only sent where you told the file picker to put it — Tbone Apps never receives or stores a copy of it.

Purchase information: Premium unlock purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

We do not collect: your browsing history, the pages you visit, or any record of your web activity is not transmitted to us or stored on our servers. Recovery Browser does not require an account, and we never ask for your name, email address, or any other personal information to use the app.

Permissions the App Uses

Internet access: Used only to load the websites you choose to visit in the browser. Recovery Browser does not use this to send your data anywhere.

Notifications: Used to show download progress when you download a file through the browser.

Storage (older Android versions only): Used so you can download files and upload files (for example, attaching a photo on a website) on Android versions that require this permission for file access.

Billing: Used to process premium unlock purchases through Google Play Billing.

Recovery Browser does not use Android's Accessibility Service, and does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location, or any other sensitive permission beyond what's listed above.

Third-Party Services

Google Play Billing: Processes premium unlock payments under its own privacy terms.

Recovery Browser does not integrate any analytics, advertising, or tracking software.

Data Retention and Deletion

All app data (streak history, whitelist, settings) remains on your device until you uninstall the app, clear its data, or delete it yourself from within the app. Backup files you create are stored wherever you chose to save them and are entirely under your control.

Your Rights

Because Recovery Browser doesn't collect or store your data anywhere but your own device, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on our end — you already have full control through the app itself and your device's settings. If you have questions about this, contact us at tboneapps@outlook.com.

Children's Privacy

Recovery Browser is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant age of digital consent in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective date" above.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at tboneapps@outlook.com.
