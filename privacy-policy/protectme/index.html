ProtectMe Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026 · Covers ProtectMe for Android, ProtectMe for Windows, and ProtectMe Companion Browser Extension

Summary

ProtectMe is a self-control app and website blocker across Android, Windows, and desktop browsers. Everything it does happens locally on your own device. We do not run a server that collects your personal data, there are no accounts, and we do not show ads, use analytics, sell your data or track you.

Who we are

ProtectMe is published by Tbone Apps. Contact: tboneapps@outlook.com

1. ProtectMe for Android (Mobile)

Accessibility Service

ProtectMe uses Android's Accessibility Service API so it can do the job you set it up for:

  • See which app is open, so it can block the apps you chose.
  • Spot when the Google app's search is used, if you turned on Block Google Search.
  • Stop protection being switched off or the app being removed from Settings while protection is on.

This information is checked on your device the moment it happens and is never stored, logged or sent anywhere. ProtectMe does not read your messages, passwords or other personal content, and does not use the Accessibility Service for any other purpose.

VPN (Apps offline)

If you put apps in Apps offline, ProtectMe starts a local VPN on your device to cut those apps off from the internet. The VPN runs entirely on your phone. It does not send your traffic to any server, and it does not inspect, record or share your browsing or app data. Apps you have not chosen are not affected.

Information the Android app uses

  • Your app list. ProtectMe reads the list of installed apps so you can choose which to block or take offline. Your choices are saved on your device.
  • Unlock keys. If you add a password, QR code, barcode or NFC tag as an unlock key, it is stored securely on your device so ProtectMe can check it. It is never sent to us.
  • Camera. Used only to scan a QR code or barcode key. Camera images are processed on your device and are never saved or sent anywhere.
  • NFC. Used only to read an NFC tag you use as an unlock key.
  • Device admin. Used only to stop ProtectMe being uninstalled while protection is on. It is not used to read, wipe or lock your device.
  • Schedules and settings. Your protection schedule and other settings are saved on your device.

2. ProtectMe for Windows (Desktop)

  • Local Windows Service. ProtectMe runs a local background Windows Service (ProtectMeService) solely to close blocked applications, enforce your chosen schedules, and prevent unauthorized uninstallation while protection is active.
  • Local Website Whitelist Proxy & Browser Policies. When Website Protection, Safe Browsing, or Block Image & Video Search is enabled, ProtectMe routes traffic through a local loopback proxy (127.0.0.1) and applies local Windows browser policies. No browsing history or traffic is ever sent to Tbone Apps or any third party.
  • Master Password & Configuration. Your Master Password is stored locally on your PC as a salted PBKDF2-SHA256 hash in C:\ProgramData\ProtectMe\config.json. It is never transmitted off your device.
  • Optional License Key Verification. If you purchase Websites Pro via Gumroad, ProtectMe sends only your license key to Gumroad's verification API (api.gumroad.com) to confirm validity. No account is required.
  • Update Checks. On startup, ProtectMe checks GitHub Releases to notify you when a newer version is available.

3. ProtectMe Companion Browser Extension

The ProtectMe Companion extension (for Chrome, Brave, Edge, and compatible Chromium browsers) works alongside ProtectMe for Windows:

  • Single Purpose. Applies local CSS styling to render images and videos as solid black boxes and pauses video playback while ProtectMe Safe Browsing is active.
  • Zero Data Collection. The extension does not collect, store, log, sell, or transmit any browsing history, webpage content, personal information, or analytics data. Everything runs 100% locally in your browser.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, email address or phone number.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics tools in the app.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • When protection is off, you can change or remove your blocked apps, offline apps and unlock keys at any time.
  • Turning protection off and uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your device.
  • You can turn off Accessibility, VPN, camera or other permissions in your phone's settings when protection is off. ProtectMe needs them to work.

Children

ProtectMe is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the date above.
