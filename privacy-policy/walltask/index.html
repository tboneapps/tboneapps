WallTask Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how WallTask ("WallTask", "the app") collects, uses, and protects information. WallTask is developed by Tbone Apps.

WallTask

Developer: Tbone Apps

Contact: tboneapps@outlook.com

Last Updated: 22 August 2026

What WallTask Does

WallTask turns your to-do list into your phone's wallpaper and home screen widget — check off a task and watch your original wallpaper come back into view as the list clears.

Data Stored On Your Device

Your tasks, theme, and display preferences are stored locally on your device using a private, on-device database. This data is never sent to us or to any third party, and we have no access to it.

Google Drive Backup (Optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google and use the "Back up to Drive" / "Restore from Drive" feature, WallTask requests the drive.file permission scope. This scope restricts the app to only the files it creates itself — WallTask cannot see, list, or access any other file in your Google Drive.

Under this feature, WallTask creates and manages a single file, walltask_backup.json, containing your task list and display preferences. This file is stored in your own Google Drive account, under your control, and can be deleted by you at any time from Google Drive. We do not access, read, or store a copy of this file ourselves — it is transferred directly between your device and your Google Drive account.

Manual Backup

The "Export backup to file" / "Restore from file" feature writes or reads a plain JSON file at a location you choose on your device (or a cloud storage folder you select through Android's file picker). This file is never transmitted to us.

Purchase Information

Premium subscriptions are handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

What We Do Not Collect

WallTask does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. We do not collect your name, contact list, location, or browsing activity.

Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored data (tasks, preferences) remains on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Any Drive backup file is retained until you delete it yourself from Google Drive.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at tboneapps@outlook.com. If you're located in the UK/EEA, you have rights under UK GDPR/GDPR including data portability and the right to object to processing.

Children's Privacy

WallTask is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant age of digital consent in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at tboneapps@outlook.com.
