Tbone Browser Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Tbone Browser ("Tbone Browser", "the app") collects, uses, and protects information. Tbone Browser is developed by Tbone Apps.

Tbone Browser

Developer: Tbone Apps

Contact: tboneapps@outlook.com

Last Updated: 2 August 2026

What Tbone Browser Does

Tbone Browser is a whitelist-only web browser. Every site not on your personal whitelist is blocked by default. Optional tools — a tamper-resistant Commitment Timer, a Friend/Parent Lock, a Daily Browser Timer, and a permanent adult-content block — help you stay on the sites you chose to allow.

Information We Collect

Whitelist and browsing data: Your whitelist entries, Whitelist Page Only exceptions, shortcuts, bookmarks, and browsing history are stored locally on your device to run the app's core blocking functionality.

Timer and lock settings: Commitment Timer and Daily Browser Timer durations, and the Friend/Parent Lock password, are stored locally on your device. The Friend/Parent Lock password is never transmitted off your device.

Backups: If you use Backup & Restore, your whitelist, shortcuts, bookmarks, history, offline pages, saved passwords, settings, and premium status are saved to a single backup file that you control and store yourself.

Purchase information: Subscription purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing, Amazon Appstore, or Apple's In-App Purchase (StoreKit), depending on where you downloaded the app. We do not receive or store your payment card details — purchase validation is handled by the respective platform.

We do not collect: the content of sites you visit, or any data unrelated to app functionality.

Adult Content Blocking

Tbone Browser permanently blocks access to pornography and adult content sites, regardless of whitelist status or whether a Commitment Timer is running. This blocking happens entirely on-device; no browsing activity is sent to us or a third party to enforce it.

Third-Party Services

Billing providers: Google Play Billing, Amazon Appstore, and Apple StoreKit each process payments under their own privacy terms.

Data Retention and Deletion

All data described above is stored locally on your device and remains there until you uninstall the app, clear its data, or delete it yourself. Backup files you create are retained wherever you choose to store them.

Your Rights

You may request information about, or deletion of, any data by contacting us at tboneapps@outlook.com. If you're located in the UK/EEA, you have rights under UK GDPR/GDPR including data portability and the right to object to processing.

Children's Privacy

Tbone Browser is designed to support parental controls and may be used by parents to help supervise children's device usage. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents remain responsible for supervising their children's online activity.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at tboneapps@outlook.com.
