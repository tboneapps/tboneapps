Video Merger: No Watermark Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Video Merger: No Watermark ("the app") handles information. The app is developed by Tbone Apps.

Video Merger: No Watermark

Developer: Tbone Apps

Contact: tboneapps@outlook.com

Last Updated: 6 August 2026

What This App Does

Video Merger: No Watermark is a video trimming and merging tool. You pick video clips already stored on your device, trim and reorder them, choose transitions between clips, and merge them into a single output video. All video processing — trimming, transitions, and merging — happens entirely on your device using on-device video processing libraries. Your videos are never uploaded anywhere.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not know who you are, and we do not receive any data from your use of the app.

Videos and project data: The video clips you add, the projects you create, and your trim/transition choices are stored only in the app's local storage and local database on your device. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

Device permissions: The app requests access to your device's videos (Photos and videos / Storage permission) solely so you can select clips to edit and so the finished merged video can be saved back to your device's gallery. The app does not access any other files, contacts, location, or device identifiers.

Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. Video processing is performed using open-source, on-device libraries (including FFmpeg-based components) that run locally within the app and do not transmit any data externally.

Data Storage and Deletion

All project data, trimmed clips, and merged output videos are stored locally on your device. Deleting a project within the app, or uninstalling the app entirely, removes this locally stored data. Merged videos you've saved to your device's gallery remain there (like any other photo or video) until you delete them yourself, since they are managed by your device's own gallery/media storage, not by us.

Your Rights

Because the app does not collect or transmit any personal data to us, there is no personal data held by Tbone Apps for this app to access, correct, or delete on your behalf. If you have any questions or concerns, you can still contact us at tboneapps@outlook.com.

Children's Privacy

Video Merger: No Watermark is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant age of digital consent in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children — the app does not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at tboneapps@outlook.com.
